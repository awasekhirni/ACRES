#Agentic Climate Risk Engine & Suite (ACRES)
- where climate risk becomes credit-grade intelligence 


Every loan a bank carries a hidden question: what happens to this borrower when the climate changes?
For decades , that quesion lived in a separate room where sustainability teams wrote reports, risk teams ran credit models, and they rarely met.
ACRES exists to close this gap.

ACRES is an agentic AI platform that runs climate risk such as physical, transition, nature, and tipping-point into the numbers 
that actualy move a balance sheet: probability of default, loss given default, exposure at default, and IFRS 9 expected credit loss.
It does this not by bolting a green label into existing models, but by rebuilding the analytical chain from the ground up, with climate as a first-class input at every step.
